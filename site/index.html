This website is dedicated to all things Flamingo & Cactus.

Who we are: 

At Flamingo and Cactus we aim to provide the best possible services to as many people as possible. To achieve this, we work on open source first principles, releasing everything we can about everything we do, this provides transparency, keeping everyone on the same playing field. 


What we can do:

We are very flexible with our services, and always happy to learn new skills. We currently specialize in PCB design, Electrical maintenance, and Electrical unit construction. We have the most experience in solar systems installation, and general landscaping/yard work.  For any needs or desires please contact us to get a quick quote for any work. 


Our goal:

It is our belief that by applying transparency and fair values to our work, we can create a truly amazing group, made for and by the people.
