About this form

This form sends each note once, through FormInit (forminit.com), a third-party form service, and FormInit emails it to flamingoncactusgroup@gmail.com. The page never retries a send automatically, so one note cannot become two emails. If FormInit does not confirm, the page says so and points you at the direct email address instead. Files are limited to 10 MB in total. With JavaScript turned off, the plain form posts once through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and lands on a thank-you page. Your text stays in your browser while you type, so a failed send does not lose your draft.
