This website is dedicated to all things Flamingo & Cactus.

This is the Lamoka1/2 inverter, our largest project to date, which turns 12 V DC into 120 V AC. It's a open project so its hardware and firmware are built in the open every file being hosted on GitHub.

Who we are

At Flamingo and Cactus we aim to provide the best possible services to as many people as possible. To achieve this, we work on open source first principles, releasing everything we can about everything we do, this provides transparency, keeping everyone on the same playing field. 

What we can do

We are very flexible with our services, and always happy to learn new skills. We currently specialize in PCB design, and Electrical unit construction. Our solar system installation experience is limited to low power systems. For any needs or desires please contact us to get a quick quote for any work. 

Our goal

It is our belief that by applying transparency and fair values to our work, we can create a truly amazing group, made for and by the people. 

Built in the open

This website is open source too. The builder, the page cleaner, the Flank ledger generator and the chart code are all in the same repository, with the history of every change behind them. If something here is wrong, you can read exactly how it works and show us the fix.
