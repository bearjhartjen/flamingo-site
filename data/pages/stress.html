Stress City

Stress City is where we keep an honest gauge of how much is on the group's plate. One train runs through it: the Stress Express. Where the train is — parked, boarding, entering, departing, at full speed, or overloaded — tells you how overwhelmed we are right now, and the board below tracks it as a number too.
