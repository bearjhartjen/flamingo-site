The great fall of the Lamoka1/2 assembly in Oct 2026:

We were trying to order components from Digikey to get sent to China at PCBways warehouse for assembly of the Lamoka1/2. Digikey was having some website and credit card transaction issues, then of course, international routing logistics problems came up. We had to cancel these orders completely as they would not arrive on time for the fair and would require us to through unnecessary means of payment. We tried Mouser after that, more tiring website errors came up (of course) and we couldn't get verification that anything would work to our needs. Our battle plan has now changed to presenting a raw (unassembled) laminated sandwich of the Lamoka1/2 at the Owego Rotary Craft fair. Then we will figure out all logistics to get the Lamoka1/2 completely ready for purchase, get one ourselves, test it out, then upload it to PCBway share projects community.
