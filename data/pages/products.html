The Lamoka1/2

This is Flamingo & Cactuses biggest and most advanced product yet, it will take a 11-14V input (Voltage divider on AIN0 feeds voltage level to our RP2354A MCU through the ADS7128), pass it all through a 2mΩ shunt (RCS1 feeding current data to our RP2354A MCU via a INA240A2DR component to AIN4) This CSP rail will go to a buck converter for our 5V rail (TPS62160DSGR) which powers the units fan and gets stepped down to 3v3 (AMS1117-3.3) for control components. Alongside 5V the CSP rail goes to a boost converter circuit controlled by an LM5122 (LM5122MHX/NOPB running its own current through a second 2mΩ Rsense1), this steps up to 21.75V (Voltage divider on AIN1 feeds voltage level to MCU through the ADS7128, set by 64.9k/3.79k), then that goes to a high current H-bridge (4x CSD19536KCS FETs driven from GPIO1-4 through 2x UCC27735 drivers, midpoints read back on AIN2/AIN3) which drives the 12V winding of our off-board VPT24-6670 transformer on BLUE/BROWN and comes out as 120V RMS AC on BLACK/RED to the outlet.

The Lamoka1/2 is a product under the Lamoka1-project, a project that aims to create the best possible inverter/charger unit through openness and diverse perspective. The Lamoka1/2 is simply the Lamoka1 without charging functionality. All of the  information and filing of the Lamoka1/2 can be found here at our Github repository.


The Lamoka1-DEV-Brain
