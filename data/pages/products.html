The Lamoka1/2

Flamingo & Cactus's biggest and most advanced product yet. It takes an 11–14 V input and turns it into 120 V RMS AC at the outlet:

  • 11–14 V in on the CSP rail: current through a 2 mΩ shunt (RCS1) read by an INA240A2DR to AIN4, voltage on AIN0, both through the ADS7128 to our RP2354A MCU
  • TPS62160 buck makes 5 V for the unit's fan, stepped down to 3.3 V (AMS1117-3.3) for control
  • LM5122MHX/NOPB boost (second 2 mΩ Rsense1) steps up to a 21.75 V bus, set by 64.9 k / 3.79 k and read back on AIN1
  • Four CSD19536KCS FETs in an H-bridge, driven from GPIO1–4 through two UCC27735 drivers, midpoints read back on AIN2/AIN3
  • Off-board VPT24-6670 transformer, driven on BLUE/BROWN, 120 V RMS AC out on BLACK/RED

All of the information and filing of the Lamoka1/2 can be found here at our GitHub repository.
