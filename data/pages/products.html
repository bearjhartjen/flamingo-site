The Lamoka1/2

This is Flamingo & Cactus's biggest and most advanced product yet. In plain terms, it takes power from a battery (11 to 14 volts) and turns it into 120 volt RMS AC power — the same kind that comes out of a wall outlet in America — so you can plug normal things into it. The short version of how it works: the battery power is measured as it comes in, boosted up to a higher voltage, then switched by an H-bridge circuit through the transformer and out to the outlet.

The full technical explanation (the long version from the original write-up) is on our deep explanations page here.

The Lamoka1/2 is a product under the Lamoka1-project, a project that aims to create the best possible inverter/charger unit through openness and diverse perspectives. The Lamoka1/2 is simply the Lamoka1 without charging functionality. All of the  information and filing of the Lamoka1/2 can be found here at our GitHub repository.
