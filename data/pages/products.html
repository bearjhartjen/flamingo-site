The Lamoka1/2

This is Flamingo & Cactus's biggest and most advanced product yet. This unit takes power from a  DC source (11 to 14 volts) and turns it into 120 volt RMS AC form. An example of this units usage would be in a car, where normally you don't have average North-American wall outlet power, with this unit you could. The possibilities really are endless for this unit, users can run whatever firmware they might want, and with our direct MOSFET-MCU connection, this means, any waveform can be generated! 

The Lamoka1/2 is a product under the Lamoka1-project, a project that aims to create the best possible inverter/charger unit through openness and diverse perspectives. The Lamoka1/2 is simply the Lamoka1 without charging functionality. All of the  information and filing of the Lamoka1/2 can be found here at our GitHub repository.


The Lamoka1-DEV-Brain

Our first finished product, this is a development board used for firmware and control analysis. The board features ten RGB lights, and a push button. All of the  information and filing of the Lamoka1-DEV-Brain can be found here at our GitHub repository.
