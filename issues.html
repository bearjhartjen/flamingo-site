Much too expensive to be considered reasonable. Beginning of Nov 2026:

When going through to order the Lamoka1/2 with turnkey component assembly, that would be used as a beginning prototype and fully assembled proof of concept, we got a quote from PCBWay saying total assembly/components would cost $373.71 (components making up for 92.23% of that toal). This is a huge issue as we want this unit to be available to as many people possible, this requires at very least, reasonable pricing. This huge price for components is not PCBWays fault at all, it is our fault for picking the top tier component manufacturers without real reason. It is very possible that we order this very expensive board ourselves anyways, as then we can test the product with shaky/untested firmware, we think the more expensive higher quality components may hold up better to mistakes. That was not our original intention for our testing unit however, but it seems to make the most sense now. We will quickly revise and turn out an orderable inverter, at a fair price. We have our minds set on high quality, but there is a line and a middle ground that can be reached while we are still depending very heavily on others. 


The great fall of the Lamoka1/2 assembly in Oct 2026:

We were trying to order components from Digikey to get sent to China at PCBways warehouse for assembly of the Lamoka1/2 by the time of the Craft fair. Digikey was having some website (all issues were pointed out to Digikey) and credit card transaction issues, then of course, international routing logistics problems came up. We had to cancel these orders completely as they would only arrive post fair and would require us to go through unnecessary means of payment. We tried Mouser after that, more tiring website errors came up (also reported to our best ability) and we couldn't get verification that any method would work to our needs. Our battle plan has now changed to presenting a raw (unassembled) laminated sandwich of the Lamoka1/2 at the Owego Rotary Craft fair. Then we will figure out all logistics to get the Lamoka1/2 completely ready for purchase, get one ourselves, test it out, then upload it to PCBway share projects community.
